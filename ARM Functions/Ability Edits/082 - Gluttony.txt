Ability: Gluttony (ID: 82)
==================================================

Effect:
Makes the Pokémon eat any held Berry triggered by low HP below 1/2 its max HP.

Dynamic ARM Mechanic:
- **Item Hook:** The logic hooks into the item consumption/removal subroutines. It compares the held item ID against known item index ranges (e.g., the Berry IDs) to determine if the effect should trigger.

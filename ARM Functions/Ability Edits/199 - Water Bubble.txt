Ability: Water Bubble (ID: 199)

Halves damage from Fire moves, doubles damage of Water moves, and prevents burns.

- **Status Affliction/Check:** The code loads the specific bitflag representing the status condition (e.g., `0x8` for Poison, `0x10` for Burn) into a register. - **Immunity Bypass:** The code sets a global immunity flag in memory (`str rX, [rY]`) OR it executes a branch (`b` or `beq`) that literally jumps completely over the damage/status calculation subroutine, thereby preventing the effect from occurring.

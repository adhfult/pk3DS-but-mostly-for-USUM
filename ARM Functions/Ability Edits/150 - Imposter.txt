Ability: Imposter (ID: 150)
==================================================

Effect:
Transforms upon entering battle.

Dynamic ARM Mechanic:
- **Form ID Override:** The engine writes a new integer to the Form ID byte in the Pokémon's data struct and triggers the form-change visual subroutine to update its model and stats.

0x1AFA0: 0400A0E1  mov	r0, r4

Logic Translation:
- `0x1AF94`: Compares the loaded ability ID against 0x96. (Multiple cmp instructions in a row mean the engine is checking if the Pokémon has one of several abilities that share the exact same mechanic, like Mold Breaker/Teravolt/Turboblaze).
